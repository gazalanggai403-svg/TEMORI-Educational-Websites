Functional breakfast bar

Start the day stronger.

TEMORI is a plant-based functional breakfast bar concept for adolescent nutrition.

Developed as a practical nutritional intervention, TEMORI combines locally available ingredients with protein, iron, and dietary fiber — built for growing bodies with busy mornings.

Explore TEMORI ↓ Meet the researchers

Introduction

What is TEMORI?

🥜

Plant-based

Designed using locally sourced plant ingredients to provide a practical, convenient breakfast option.

🩸

Nutritional focus

Concentrated on supporting adequate intake of protein, iron, and fiber — nutrients particularly relevant to adolescent nutrition.

🌱

Local innovation

By prioritizing accessible ingredients, TEMORI shows how local food resources can become functional products.

Why it matters

Nutrition should be practical.

Adolescence is a period of rapid growth. A convenient food product can help make nutritious choices easier to reach — especially when busy schedules make it easy to skip breakfast.

Key focus: protein, iron & fiber

Protein

Supports normal growth and maintenance of body tissues.

Iron

An essential micronutrient involved in normal oxygen transport in the body.

Dietary fiber

Contributes to a balanced diet and supports normal digestive function.

Important note TEMORI is presented as a functional food concept and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or replace professional medical care for anemia or other nutritional conditions. Actual nutritional values should be confirmed through laboratory analysis of the final formulation.

From ingredients to bar

How TEMORI is developed

01

Ingredient preparation

Selected ingredients are prepared and processed into suitable forms.

02

Mixing

Ingredients are combined according to the intended formulation.

03

Molding & baking

The mixture is shaped into bars and processed into the final product.

04

Cooling & packaging
